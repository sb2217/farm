Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer bulk pricing for large orders?

Yes. For orders above 500 kg, we offer tiered bulk pricing. Please contact us via phone or WhatsApp with your quantity and crop type for a custom quote.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. We ship to all pin codes across India via our logistics partners. Real-time tracking is provided via SMS.

Are your products safe for organic certification?

Absolutely. All KisanMart products are NPOP certified and approved for use in certified organic farms. Certificate copies are available upon request.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the product is damaged or not as described. Cash on Delivery and prepaid orders are both eligible for returns.
